## Descripción (1257/4000)
Liora is an EPUB reader built so you read a little every day and keep the habit.

When you start, you choose what you want from reading, how many minutes a day (5, 10, 20 or 30) and when: morning, afternoon or night, at the time and on the days you prefer. After that, you just read. Liora keeps your reading time and compares it with your goal.

NO ACCOUNT, NO ADS
There is no sign-up, no login and no ads. Your library and your progress stay on your phone.

YOUR LIBRARY
• Import your own EPUB books.
• Or download one from the Liora catalog.
• Liora blocks duplicates, and you can delete anything you no longer want.

A READER MADE TO KEEP YOU READING
• Pick up exactly where you left off.
• Light or dark mode, scrolling or pages.
• Type size that fits you.
• Save quotes and share them.
• The reading session pauses when you leave the app.

YOUR CONSISTENCY, DAY BY DAY
See your week against your goal and a calendar of the days you read. You can change your habit any time from your profile.

LIORA PLUS (ONE-TIME PAYMENT)
The essentials are free. With one payment, no subscription, Liora Plus unlocks your month in review, time per book and the reader's appearance.
