# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

A reader who speaks Spanish and English, on their own phone, building a daily reading habit. They want to read a little and keep going. There is no account.

## Product Purpose

Liora is a reader. The job is to read a little each day and sustain that habit.

The reader is the central function. Engines sit around it. The habit engine is the one that exists. A second engine, for learning English while reading, is intended and not built. Success is a habit that holds: a chosen amount of reading, at a chosen time, repeated.

## Positioning

The habit engine around the reader. A neighboring EPUB app can open a book; it cannot truthfully claim this habit engine as its own. The planned English-learning engine is a second wrap around the same reader, and it is not part of the product yet.

## Operating Context

The person uses Liora on their phone. First run asks what they want from reading, how many minutes, and when (morning, afternoon, or night; an hour; which days). After that they import an EPUB, or take a book from the hosted catalog, and read. Time read is kept against the daily goal, on a week chart and a calendar. The habit can be edited later from the profile.

Books and progress stay on this device. The interface copy in the app today is Spanish. The home-screen name on iPhone and Android is Liora.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed today:

- No account. Library and reading progress are stored on the device.
- Onboarding, required before the app, and editable afterward: motivations (read more, create a habit, finish more books, learn new things, a moment for oneself), a daily goal of 5, 10, 20, or 30 minutes, and a routine of period, hour, and weekdays.
- A local library: import an EPUB, refuse a duplicate, delete a book.
- A hosted catalog the person can download into that library.
- A Readium reader: place in the book, dark or light, scroll or pages, type size, share, and a reading session that pauses when the app is backgrounded.
- A time view: weekly reading against the goal, a calendar, and time per book.

Intended, not built, and not specified beyond the intent: an engine to learn English while reading. Mechanism, scope, and when it ships are open.

Open: whether the interface itself is offered in English as well as Spanish. Whether “Generar lecturas de ejemplo” on the profile (random sample sessions for the past month and a half) is a product feature or a development aid.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Liora. The current welcome states the promise in Spanish: “Crea el hábito de leer.” “Un lugar para leer un poco cada día y sostener ese hábito.” “Sin cuenta. Tus libros y tu progreso se guardan en este dispositivo.”

The in-app window title is still “Lector.”

## Evidence on Hand

The evidence is the app itself: Spanish interface copy, the onboarding choices, and the library, catalog, reader, time, and profile flows. There is no marketing site, testimonial, case study, press, or pricing to reuse. Do not invent any of those. Do not describe the English-learning engine as if it already works.

## Product Principles

- The reader is the center. Engines wrap it; they do not replace it.
- A little reading, at a chosen time, is the habit the product is for.
- No account. The library and the progress stay on the phone.
- Speak to a reader whose life includes Spanish and English.
- Only the habit engine is real until the English-learning engine is specified and built.
